# Sky replacement and day for night

Strategy: 2D, or 2.5D for moving cameras. Stages: 04 (if moving), 09, 10.

Steps:
1. Key or roto the sky; soft edges on trees and hair.
2. Moving camera: track it; put the new sky on a far card or a dome so parallax is right.
3. Match the new sky's light: warm or cool the land to the sky colour, match sun direction.
4. Day for night: night sky, darken the land heavily (bright peaks most), add a motivated back light to subjects, cool grade, keep some shadow detail.
5. Lightning: flash the land, subject and fog with the sky brightness per frame.

Checks: no edge halos; the land's light direction agrees with the new sky.
