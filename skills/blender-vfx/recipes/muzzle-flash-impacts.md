# Muzzle flashes, sparks, bullet hits

Strategy: 2D. Stages: 09.

Shoot: actor fires a prop that cannot fire; mark each trigger frame with a clap or note. For hits, a held marker at the impact point to paint out.

Steps:
1. Mark trigger frames. One flash lasts 1 to 2 frames.
2. Stock flash elements, scaled to the barrel, 2D tracked to the muzzle.
3. Interactive light: brighten the actor's face and nearby walls for those frames, warm colour.
4. Hits: dust or spark element at the impact, a small hole decal tracked to the surface, debris falling with gravity.
5. Camera shake of 2 to 4 frames on each shot.
6. Grain over everything.

Checks: light on the actor matches the flash frame; no flash on frames without recoil.
