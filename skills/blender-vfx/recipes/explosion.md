# Explosion

Strategy: stock elements first, plus a small sim where it touches CG or the actor. Stages: 07, 08, 09.

Steps:
1. Track the plate if the camera moves.
2. Main blast: stock explosion elements placed and scaled in 3D (cards) or 2D.
3. Small Mantaflow sim only for interaction: smoke wrapping a CG object or debris near camera.
4. Debris: rigid body pieces or stock debris; dust layers between and in front.
5. Composite: fire warmer and glowing, smoke with colour variation, light on the plate from the fireball, lifted blacks if the plate's blacks are lifted.
6. Shockwave: distortion and dust ring, camera shake timed to arrival.

Checks: light on the surroundings peaks with the fireball; smoke is not one flat colour; shake arrives after the flash for distant blasts.
