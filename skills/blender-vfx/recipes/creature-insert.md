# Creature or character in the plate

Strategy: 3D matchmove. Stages: 02, 03, 04, 05, 06, 08, 09.

Shoot: a stand-in or eyeline marker at the creature's height (drone at head height for giants), clean plate, grey and chrome ball, measured ground distance.

Steps:
1. Track and solve; rebuild ground and anything the creature touches.
2. Buy or download a detailed model; add displacement for close ups; scale to its real size.
3. Animate from reference: weight, secondary motion, breathing.
4. Light with a matching HDRI plus a motivated rim light for contrast.
5. Shadow catcher on the ground, holdouts for foreground plants and rocks.
6. Contact: dust puffs on footfalls (small sim or stock), grass over the feet as a matte.
7. Composite: match blacks, defocus, motion blur, atmosphere layers in front and behind, grain.

Checks: no foot sliding; shadows match the sun; creature as soft as the plate; scale reads against people.
