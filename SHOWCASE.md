## Submit your shot

1. Fill in the [shot form](https://github.com/AryannDhingraa/blender-vfx-skill/issues/new?template=shot-submission.yml), or post it with **#BlenderVFXSkill** and link the post in the form.
2. Include the before and the after from the same clip, your setup, render time and what you fixed by hand.

**Requirements:** your own footage (or footage you may share), a yes from everyone in it, and rights to any music or stock in it.

**Permission and credit:** you keep ownership of your footage and your work. Permission is non-exclusive and covers only the places you tick in the form (showcase, social media, promotional videos). Every feature credits you the way you asked. To withdraw, comment on your issue and the shot comes down from the showcase and is left out of future posts. The maintainer still checks permissions before featuring anything; the form's ticks are a record, not a substitute for that.
